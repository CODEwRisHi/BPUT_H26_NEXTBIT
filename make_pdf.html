Layer 1: Sender & Channel Verification (Modules 01 - 06)
  1. Official Sender Check: Confirms messages arrive from genuine brand sender IDs (like AX-FLPKRT) instead of spoofed numbers.
  2. Random Sender Identifier: Identifies bot-generated, randomized alphanumeric sender masks.
  3. Lookalike Letter Detector: Catches fake URLs substituting Latin characters with identical foreign Unicode glyphs.
  4. Email Signature Verifier: Validates SPF, DKIM, and DMARC cryptographic signatures before body inspection.
  5. Invisible Character Scrubber: Strips hidden zero-width characters used to bypass keyword filters.
  6. Connection Hop Tracker: Traces routing hops to flag visitors proxying through Tor relays or VPN chains.
Layer 2: Message Context & Extortion Detection (Modules 07 - 12)
  1. Safe OTP Inspector: Distinguishes normal delivery arrival pins from requests coercing verbal disclosure.
  2. Fake Fee Detector: Detects small fee demands (e.g., ₹10 parcel redelivery charges) designed to harvest cards.
  3. Rogue App Downloader Flag: Detects prompts instructing users to download external .apk files.
  4. False Urgency Scanner: Flags psychological panic triggers such as "Blocked in 15 minutes."
  5. Order Cross-Referencing: Checks delivery tracking codes against active, expected shipments.
  6. Impersonation Detector: Detects impersonation of police, customs, tax officials, or delivery coordinators.
Layer 3: Audio & Voice Clone Defense (Modules 13 - 17)
  1. High-Frequency Voice Cutoff: Scans for missing sound harmonics above 14 kHz common in AI voice models.
  2. Natural Vocal Tremble Check: Evaluates pitch variations that text-to-speech generators artificially smooth over.
  3. Spectrogram Glitch Scanner: Scans sound wave spectrograms to identify phase and vocoder anomalies.
  4. Fake Ambient Noise Filter: Detects repetitive background audio loops used by fraudulent call centers.
  5. Biometric Voiceprint Match: Matches caller voice prints against verified family or executive baselines.
Layer 4: Identity & Account Security (Modules 18 - 21)
  1. Impossible Travel Alert: Flags logins from two distant geographical locations occurring minutes apart.
  2. Brute-Force Login Shield: Detects automated password-guessing bursts targeting account portals.
  3. Suspicious Server Checker: Checks IP addresses against threat intelligence lists of active botnets and proxies.
  4. Session Hijacking Guard: Flags browser or device fingerprint swaps during an active session.
Layer 5: Scoring & Automated Actions (Modules 22 - 25)
  1. Zero False-Positive Risk Calculator: Balances all module findings into a threat score from 0 to 100.
  2. Automated Defense Action: Automatically allows verified deliveries, blocks scams, and quarantines malicious links.
  3. Tamper-Proof Audit Logger: Writes audit events directly to the local activity ledger.
  4. One-Click Incident Report Exporter: Compiles comprehensive technical incident records into exportable reports.
